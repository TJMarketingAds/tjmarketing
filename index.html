FAQ

Common questions

What's your minimum ad spend?

Around $1,000/month in ad spend is where Meta has enough data to work with. Below roughly $30/day, campaigns struggle to get out of the learning phase and results stay unreliable. Your ad budget is paid straight to Meta on your own card — it's separate from my fee.

Do you guarantee a specific return?

No, and I'd be careful with anyone who does. Your return depends on your pricing, margins, how fast you follow up on leads, and how well your page converts — most of which sits on your side, not mine. What I do commit to is the work: campaigns built and launched within 7 days, weekly creative testing, weekly written reporting, and month-to-month terms so you can leave if it isn't working.

How quickly will I see something?

Campaigns are live within 7 days of signing. Meaningful data usually takes 2–4 weeks, because Meta needs a certain number of conversions before it can optimise properly. Anyone promising results in week one is guessing.

Who do you work with?

Home improvement and exterior contractors doing $3,000+ jobs — epoxy and concrete coatings, artificial turf, fencing, hardscaping, deck building, pool resurfacing, basement waterproofing, and garage storage systems. We don't work with restaurants, dentists, gyms, or ecommerce stores — staying focused on one kind of business is what makes the creative and targeting actually work.

Do you make the ad creatives?

Yes. I write all the copy and produce static and carousel ads. For video I'll either work with footage you already have or tell you exactly what to film — usually straightforward on a phone.

Am I locked into a contract?

There's a written agreement so both of us know the scope, the fee and the ad budget — but it runs month to month. Cancel with notice and we stop at the end of the month. No annual lock-in.
